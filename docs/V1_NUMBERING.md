# arXiv 2305.15625v1: numbering correspondence

The repository's `README.md`, `FORMALIZATION.md` and `docs/PAPER_FIDELITY.md` use
the numbering of a revised manuscript (14 July 2026). That manuscript is not
available. All checks against the source use arXiv 2305.15625v1 (May 2023,
`main.tex`, 2146 lines). This file gives the correspondence.

Conventions.

- "v1" numbers are those printed by v1. `header.sty` declares `theorem`, `lemma`,
  `corollary`, `definition`, `result` as independent `\newtheorem` counters, so
  v1 has Lemma 1 and Corollary 1 and Definition 1 side by side. The numbers below
  were obtained by counting the `\begin{...}` environments of `main.tex` in order
  (environments with the thmtools `restate=` key count once, at their first occurrence).
- "line" is the line of the `\begin{...}` of the environment in v1 `main.tex`.
- Repo numbers are matched to v1 by content, then checked against the repo's own
  docstrings.
- Definition numbers coincide in the two manuscripts (Definitions 1-21).
- "Lean" names the principal declaration of the repo number. It may be conditional
  or corrected; the status of each is in `README.md` and `docs/PAPER_FIDELITY.md`,
  and v1 defects are in `docs/V1_ERRATA.md`. "none" means no declaration states
  the result.
